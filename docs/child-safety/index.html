Varta Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Varta has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Prohibited Content and Behaviour

Users must not create, upload, distribute, request, promote, or facilitate content or behaviour involving the sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming, trafficking, sextortion, or sexualisation of minors.

Child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including visual depictions involving minors in sexually explicit conduct, is strictly prohibited.

Reporting

Varta provides in-app reporting tools that allow users to report users and user-generated content that may violate these standards.

Child-safety concerns may also be reported directly to: kemisto17@gmail.com

Enforcement

When Varta becomes aware of content or behaviour that violates these standards, appropriate action may include removal of content, restriction or termination of accounts, preservation of relevant information where legally required, and reporting to appropriate authorities.

Legal Compliance

Varta complies with applicable child-safety laws and regulations. Where legally required, confirmed instances of CSAM will be reported to the appropriate regional or national authority.
