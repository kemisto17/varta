Delete Your Varta Account

Varta users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

How to request deletion

Send an email from the email address associated with your Varta account to:

kemisto17@gmail.com

Use the subject: Varta Account Deletion

Include your Varta username so that we can identify the correct account.

What will be deleted

After the request is verified and processed, we will delete or remove personal data associated with the account, including applicable:

  • Account and profile information
  • Profile images
  • Student verification information
  • Posts and uploaded post media
  • Comments and likes
  • Organization follows and event interests
  • Push notification tokens and preferences

Information that may be retained

Limited information may be retained where reasonably necessary for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or enforcement purposes.

Information retained for these purposes will not be used to continue providing the deleted account as an active Varta account.

Contact

Questions about account deletion can be sent to kemisto17@gmail.com.
